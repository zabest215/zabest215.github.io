Flagship Project

Single-Axis Solar Tracker

Système de Suivi Solaire à Axe Unique (PFE)

An automated solar tracking system designed to maximize energy harvesting efficiency by following the sun's trajectory.

  • Smart Reset Logic: The Arduino motor logic is programmed to automatically return the tracker to the "East" position at sunset, preparing it for the next day.
  • Mechanical Simulation: The attached solar panel on the prototype serves as a mechanical weight simulation to test motor torque, rather than an electrical measurement device.
  • Tech Stack: Arduino, C++, LDR Sensors, Servo Motors.
